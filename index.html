Disclaimer

Please note: very little of this data has yet been independently verified, and I am aware of deficiencies in correctly summing up the licensed occupancy amounts for “combo listings” with multiple licenses listed in the listing description.

There are also some listings which had unique formatting at the time that I analyzed the pages, and so some licenses were not detected.

The number of occupancy violations is likely to be lower than measured, along with the number of properties without a license listed.

While I work on perfecting the detection methods, this is for entertainment or informative purposes only. All data should be independently verified before taking actions on the data. It is provided in the hope that it may be helpful, but the veracity of the data cannot yet be guaranteed. As I only work on this in my free time, I expect to have 90% complete and verifiable data by November 2026.
